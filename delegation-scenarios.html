A research study

How much would you hand to AI?

As AI tools take on more of our work, the skill that matters is deciding how much to let them do, and how closely to check the result. We are developing an assessment of that skill, and we need experienced eyes on it. You will review 30 short workplace and classroom scenarios: rate each one, choose its most and least effective response, and classify it using a short framework we will explain first.

Welcome back

About this study

  • This study is conducted by Dr. Angelo E. Thalassinidis, Anne Arundel Community College, to develop and evaluate an assessment of how people decide when and how to use AI.
  • You will answer a few questions about your work (no identifying details), read a one-page summary of the framework, and then review the scenarios. Your answers to each scenario are saved when you move to the next one, so partial responses may be used in the study. You can stop at any time by closing the page, and you can resume later on the same device and browser.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, Google account, or IP address. Results will be reported only in aggregate.
  • There are no known risks beyond those of everyday online activity, and no direct benefits. Participation is voluntary.
  • Please do not share the scenarios outside this survey; sharing them could affect the study.
  • Questions about the study: aethalassinidis@aacc.edu. Questions about your rights as a participant: AACC Institutional Review Board, priairb@aacc.edu.
